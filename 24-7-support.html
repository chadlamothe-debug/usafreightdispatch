Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really 24/7, or an after-hours answering service?

Really 24/7. You reach USA Freight Dispatch dispatchers - the same team that books your loads - at any hour, any day of the year.

What can you actually do in the middle of the night?

Reschedule appointments, notify brokers of delays, document detention, help arrange roadside assistance, and line up your next load.

Do you charge extra for after-hours support?

No. Around-the-clock support is included in the flat 7% dispatch fee.

What happens if I break down under a load?

Call us. We notify the broker, protect the load, help coordinate assistance, and manage the reschedule so one breakdown does not wreck your week.
