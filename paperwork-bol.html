Frequently Asked Questions

What documents do you handle?

Broker carrier setup packets, rate confirmations, bills of lading, proof of delivery, invoices, and the supporting documents brokers and factoring companies request.

Do you review rate confirmations before I sign?

Yes. We check the rate, accessorials, and terms on every rate con and flag anything that does not match what was negotiated.

What is a BOL and why does it matter so much?

The bill of lading is the legal document for the freight you haul. Signed correctly at pickup and delivery, it is your proof the job was done - and the key to getting paid.

Can you work with my factoring company?

Yes. We package and submit the documents your factor requires so your funding is never held up on paperwork.
