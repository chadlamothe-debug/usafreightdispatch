About USA Freight Dispatch

American owned, driver focused, and built to keep owner-operators moving — with honest rates and real people behind every load.

WHO WE ARE

Built By People
Who Know The Road

USA Freight Dispatch started with a simple belief: owner-operators and small fleets deserve a dispatch partner who is honest, dependable, and actually on their side. Too many drivers get buried in hidden fees, forced dispatch, and dispatchers they can barely reach — and we set out to be the opposite.

We handle the loads, the brokers, and the paperwork so you can keep your wheels turning and your revenue growing. One flat 7% fee, no hidden charges, and a real dispatcher who picks up the phone. That’s the whole idea.
